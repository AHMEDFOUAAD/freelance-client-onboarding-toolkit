# Freelance Client Kickoff & Onboarding Checklist

**A structured 5-phase onboarding protocol for freelance developers, technical contractors, and solo consultants.**

---

## Phase 1: Pre-Contract Qualification & Scope Alignment
- [ ] **Lead Scored with BANT:** Qualified budget, decision authority, measurable need, and verified timeline.
- [ ] **20-Minute Discovery Call:** Conducted structured interview covering business impact and technical integrations.
- [ ] **Technical Feasibility Confirmed:** Verified third-party API limits, data schemas, and architecture requirements.
- [ ] **Pricing Model Selected:** Fixed-price milestone project, monthly retainer, or day-rate block.

## Phase 2: Agreement, Statement of Work (SOW) & Deposit
- [ ] **Master Services Agreement (MSA) Issued:** Terms of service, intellectual property (IP) transfer upon full payment, warranties, and liability caps established.
- [ ] **Detailed Statement of Work (SOW):** Specific deliverables, acceptance criteria, milestone schedule, and explicit out-of-scope items defined.
- [ ] **Signatures Executed:** Legally binding e-signature secured from the authorized company officer.
- [ ] **Upfront Deposit Invoiced & Received:** 50% deposit received before scheduling kickoff or initiating work.

## Phase 3: Access Provisioning & Environment Setup
- [ ] **Access Credentials Received:** Secured via 1Password/Bitwarden or organization invite (never plaintext in chat):
  - [ ] Git repository (GitHub/GitLab) with write/admin permissions
  - [ ] Cloud infrastructure (AWS/GCP/Azure/Vercel) IAM roles or service accounts
  - [ ] Database credentials (staging / test environment, never raw production)
  - [ ] API keys, webhook endpoints, and third-party SaaS credentials (Stripe test keys, etc.)
- [ ] **Communication Channels Established:** Slack/Discord shared channel or dedicated email thread; notification SLA agreed (e.g. 24-hour response on business days).
- [ ] **Project Management Board Setup:** Notion, Linear, Trello, or GitHub Projects board initialized with milestone columns.

## Phase 4: Kickoff Alignment Meeting (30 mins)
- [ ] **Introductions & Key Contacts:** Confirm who performs acceptance testing and signs off on deliverables.
- [ ] **Sprint 1 / Milestone 1 Walkthrough:** Review the immediate 7-14 day goals, deliverables, and demo date.
- [ ] **Risk Review:** Discuss known technical dependencies, external vendor blockers, or credentials pending.
- [ ] **Weekly Async Cadence:** Confirm the format and day for weekly progress updates (e.g., Friday loom/email).

## Phase 5: Delivery, Acceptance & Offboarding
- [ ] **Staging Verification:** Deploy deliverables to staging environment with smoke-test validation.
- [ ] **Client Walkthrough Demo:** Conduct live or recorded walkthrough of completed milestones.
- [ ] **Formal Acceptance Sign-Off:** Written confirmation received approving milestone completion.
- [ ] **Final Balance Paid:** Invoiced and payment cleared prior to transferring production access and IP ownership.
- [ ] **Testimonial & Referral Request:** Send templated 3-question review request 7 days post-launch.

# Client Intake & Lead Qualification SOP

**Standard Operating Procedure for Freelance Engineers, Technical Consultants & Solo Agencies**

## 1. Purpose & Scope

This Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) outlines the exact process for qualifying inbound freelance opportunities, vetting prospective clients, and preventing unpaid discovery, non-paying clients, and scope creep before issuing a formal proposal or contract.

Applying this framework ensures you only commit proposal hours to realistic, funded, and commercially viable projects.

---

## 2. The 4-Pillar Qualification Framework (BANT-AI)

Before agreeing to a 30-minute discovery call or writing a custom statement of work (SOW), evaluate the inbound inquiry across four essential pillars:

| Pillar | Criteria | Scoring (0, 1, or 2) |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Budget** | Has the client confirmed a realistic budget ($2,000–$10,000+ depending on scope) or are they shopping for the cheapest hourly labor? | **0:** No budget / demands cheapest rate<br>**1:** Vague range<br>**2:** Explicit, funded budget |
| **2. Authority** | Are you communicating with the primary decision-maker (founder, CEO, VP) who signs contracts and approves invoices? | **0:** Gatekeeper without signing authority<br>**1:** Advisory committee<br>**2:** Direct decision-maker |
| **3. Need** | Is the business bottleneck or financial impact measurable (e.g., hours saved per week, revenue unblocked, manual errors eliminated)? | **0:** Vague idea<br>**1:** General desire to improve<br>**2:** Concrete bottleneck with measurable impact |
| **4. Timeline & Tech Readiness** | Is there a hard target launch date (< 60–90 days) and are required systems/APIs accessible? | **0:** "Whenever it's ready" / blocked access<br>**1:** Soft deadline<br>**2:** Clear timeline & existing tech stack ready |

### Action by Total Score:
- **6 to 8 points:** High-priority lead. Schedule a 20-minute structured discovery call.
- **3 to 5 points:** Incomplete inquiry. Send follow-up qualification questions via email before getting on a call.
- **0 to 2 points:** Disqualify politely using a pre-written template. Do not write custom proposals.

---

## 3. Structured Discovery Call Script & Questions

During the 20-minute discovery call, maintain control of the conversation by asking these 8 structured questions:

1. **Core Problem:** What specific operational bottleneck or technical challenge is currently causing the greatest friction in your business?
2. **Financial / Operational Impact:** What is this issue costing your company monthly in wasted employee hours, delayed deliverables, or missed customer revenue?
3. **Definition of Success:** In concrete terms, what does a successful delivery look like 30 days after launch?
4. **System Integrations:** What software, databases, or third-party APIs must this solution integrate with (e.g., Stripe, PostgreSQL, AWS, HubSpot)?
5. **Review & Acceptance:** Who on your team will test deliverables, provide consolidation feedback, and sign off on completion milestones?
6. **Timeline Driver:** What external business event drives your target launch date (e.g., product release, investor demo, quarterly deadline)?
7. **Approved Budget Allocation:** What budget range has been approved for this project ($3k–$5k, $5k–$10k, or $10k+)?
8. **Past Attempts:** Have you attempted to build or resolve this previously with internal staff or past contractors? What worked, and why did it stall?

## 4. Red Flag Disqualification Checklist

If two or more of the following conditions occur, disqualify the lead immediately or require a 100% upfront retainer before starting:

- [ ] Client refuses to discuss budget or insists: *"Give me your hourly rate first, then we'll tell you the scope."*
- [ ] Requirements change substantially between the initial message and the call (*"we will know what we want when we see it"*).
- [ ] Request for unpaid speculative work, unpaid test projects, or free proof-of-concept architectures.
- [ ] History of blaming multiple previous contractors or agencies for failed projects.
- [ ] Multiple uncoordinated stakeholders with conflicting priorities and no designated project lead.
- [ ] Artificial urgency (*"we need this delivered next week"*) coupled with multi-day communication delays on their side.

## 5. AI Assistant Lead Pre-Scoring Prompt

To quickly evaluate incoming lead emails or project briefs consistently (treat the score as a first pass, not a verdict), paste the following prompt into your LLM assistant (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini):

```markdown
You are an executive client intake assistant for a senior freelance developer/consultant.
Evaluate the following client inquiry using the BANT-AI framework:
- Budget (0 = none/unrealistic, 1 = vague, 2 = realistic & confirmed)
- Authority (0 = no decision power, 1 = committee, 2 = direct decision-maker)
- Need (0 = unclear, 1 = general, 2 = concrete bottleneck with measurable impact)
- Timeline & Readiness (0 = undefined, 1 = soft, 2 = clear deadline + tech ready)

For each pillar, assign a score and quote the exact sentence from the message that justifies it.
Check for red flags: no budget discussion, shifting scope, free spec requests, blaming past developers, artificial urgency.
Provide the 3 specific questions I must ask next before drafting an agreement.

Client Inquiry:
<PASTE CLIENT INQUIRY HERE>
```
